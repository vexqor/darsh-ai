Quick summary

  • Opening, editing, merging, splitting, compressing, protecting, signing, redacting and OCR happen on your phone. Your PDF is not uploaded for these.
  • The AI commands are optional. Only when you use them, your instruction and the needed text of your PDF are sent over the internet for processing.
  • You can use the manual tools as a guest, without any account.
  • The app shows ads (see Advertising below). Ads never read your PDFs. We do not sell your documents or use them to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account yourself from inside the app.

AI features (internet required)

When you use an AI command (for example a grammar check or an AI edit suggestion), the app asks you to confirm before the first send. Then this is sent:

  • your typed instruction, and
  • the text of your PDF needed for that command: up to about 10,000 characters per request. The full-document grammar check sends the text of every page, in batches.
📱 Your phone
☁️ PDFQuill server
🧠 Cloudflare Workers AI
💬 Answer to you

What our server keeps: a per-user and overall daily usage counter (a number) used to enforce fair-use limits. We do not store the text of your PDF or your prompts in a database, and we do not write them to our logs. Our logs only contain technical error messages.

Requests that run too long or return too much are cancelled automatically, and responses are size-limited.

⚠️

Sensitive document? Use the manual tools instead. They never leave your device. AI answers can also be wrong, so please review them before relying on them.

Account & login

An account is needed only for AI features. You can sign in with email and password, or with Google. Authentication is provided by Supabase.

  • Email sign-up: we receive your email address and a securely hashed password handled by Supabase. We never see your plain password.
  • Google sign-in: we receive your Google account email and basic profile as allowed by the Google consent screen. We don't get your Google password.
  • Sign-in uses a secure redirect back to the app (com.darsh.pdfeditor://login-callback).

Saved on your device

To help you restore work, PDFQuill can keep on your device: the last opened PDF and your edits, a recent-files list (file names and a small first-page thumbnail) and the last page you viewed.

🛡️

The saved copy is the original file, so redacted areas are not redacted in that restore copy. It is never uploaded. Turn it off in Settings → Remember last file or tap Clear saved data any time.

The app disables Android backup, so this local data is not copied to cloud backups.

Advertising

PDFQuill is free and is supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version and app version,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics.
✅

Your PDFs and AI text are never shared with advertisers. Ads are separate from your documents and run outside the editor's file content.

Your choices: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions), we ask for your consent first through a consent prompt, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. Learn more in How Google uses data from partner apps.

🤝

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication and account deletion.
  • Cloudflare: the PDFQuill server (Workers), AI processing (Workers AI) and rate limiting.
  • Google: AdMob for ads, and sign-in if you choose “Continue with Google”.

They process data only to provide these services. We do not sell your documents. Ad data is handled by the ad provider as described in the Advertising section.

🗑️

Retention & deletion

  • Delete account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and login data.
  • Local data: Settings → Clear saved data, or uninstall the app.
  • Email request: write to supportbytorra@gmail.com from your registered email and we will help.
  • Usage counters are kept only for the current day and old days are cleaned up automatically.
🛡️

Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Every AI request is authenticated, rate-limited and size-capped.
  • Imported PDFs are validated before opening; malformed files are rejected.
  • No secret server keys are stored inside the app.

No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

⚖️

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and, for users in Europe, the GDPR) you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

PDFQuill is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

FAQ

Do you upload my PDFs?

Not for manual tools. Only when you use an AI command, the needed text (not the file itself) is sent for processing.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can use all manual tools. An account is only needed for AI features.

Do you show ads or sell my data?

Yes, the free app shows ads from Google AdMob. We never share your PDFs or AI text with advertisers, and we do not sell your documents. You can opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.

How do I delete everything?

Profile → Delete account removes your account. Settings → Clear saved data removes local files and history.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Editing, OCR, scanning and all manual tools work offline. Login and AI need internet.

Questions about privacy?

We are happy to help.
